# Privacy Policy - World Cup Quiz

**Last updated: April 2026**

## 1. Overview
World Cup Quiz is a completely offline mobile application. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data from our users.

## 2. Data Collection
- We do **not** collect any personal information.
- We do **not** use analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers.
- All questions and data are generated locally on your device.
- No data is sent to any server.

## 3. Permissions
The app may request:
- Storage permission (to cache assets offline)
- Internet permission (only for initial download and optional PWA updates)

No personal data is accessed or transmitted.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from children or adults.

## 5. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify users by updating the date at the top of this page.
